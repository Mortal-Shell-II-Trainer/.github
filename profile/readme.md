## What it does

Mortal Shell II is a soulslike where you possess Shells — fallen warriors — to gain their abilities. Combat is aggressive, with Resolve replacing stamina for special moves.

This trainer removes the punishment. **God mode** makes you invincible, **unlimited resolve** lets you spam special attacks, and **one-hit kills** turns bosses into speed bumps. **Unlock all shells, weapons, and tarstones** gives you full build freedom from the start.
